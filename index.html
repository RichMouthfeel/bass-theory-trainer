Why these are all 7th chords

A bare triad doesn't say enough. C – E – G alone is common to three different scales below — they all agree on the 1st, 3rd and 5th, and only split ways once you get to the 4th and 7th degree. Until a chart tells you which of those it wants, you're guessing:

The 7th is what breaks the tie — maj7 points to Ionian (or Lydian), a plain 7 points to Mixolydian, m7 narrows a minor chord to Dorian or Aeolian instead of Phrygian or Locrian. It's also just how real charts talk to you: lead sheets and gig charts are written in 7th chords precisely so the scale choice isn't left ambiguous.
